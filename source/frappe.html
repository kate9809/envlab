What is ERPNext?

ERPNext is a 100% open-source enterprise resource planning system offering a modern, comprehensive, and user-friendly ERP experience. You are free to use, modify, and resell it, giving you complete control, flexibility, and ownership.

It works out of the box for most industries and countries and can be easily tailored to specific requirements using its built-in low-code/no-code customization tools. ERPNext covers accounting, invoicing, sales, procurement, inventory, manufacturing, projects, POS, HR, payroll, and more.

Trusted by over 30,000 companies worldwide, ERPNext is a proven solution for businesses of all sizes.
