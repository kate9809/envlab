ERP and Automation

We help businesses streamline operations by implementing ERP systems based on ERPNext and Frappe Framework, tailored to real business processes. Our service includes workflow automation, process digitization, data migration, and system integrations, allowing teams to work faster, reduce manual errors, and gain full visibility into their operations from a single, unified platform.

  • Why Integrate ERP from Day One

    In the long term, It’s more profitable and less risky to start your business with an ERP system than to begin with scattered spreadsheets and temporary tools migrating later.

  • ERP Helps Survival and Long‑Term Profitability

    Studies demonstrate that ERP implementation improves ROI for SMEs, cuts operational costs by 11% and leads to double-digit sales growth for 49% of adopters (Bharathi & Rakesh, 2012; Olowo & Kolapo, 2025).

    Additionally, ERP systems are scalable, able to grow with your business and support you through expansion.

  • ERP Eliminates Painful Migration Costs

    Moving data and processes from spreadsheets into an ERP later costs more time, money, and effort than doing it at the start.

    On average, data migration issues cause delays in 32% of ERP projects, lead to operational disruption for 40% companies and escalate internal resistance to change (Gintux).

  • ERP Improves Efficiency & Decision‑Making

    Early ERP integration avoids disjointed systems, redundant work, and manual reconciliation errors, which typically multiply as the business grows.

    Decision-making time is reduced by 36% due to centralized data in ERPs, while cycle times for key business processes improve by 30% on average (Gitnux), making the business more efficient from the start.

Implementation Case

We implemented ERPNext for a software development company, ensuring a seamless, zero-downtime migration from fragmented systems into a single, cost-effective ERP platform. The project included process auditing, staged data migration, custom workflows, and team training. As a result, the client gained a unified ERP system, reduced operational errors, and BI analytics to monitor performance and identify bottlenecks.
